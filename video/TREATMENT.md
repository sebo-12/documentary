Candidate structures
1. Keep the one continuous map flight, and give each archive object a catalogue caption (chosen).
2. Rebuild as a pure light-table film: all nine objects on one glowing table, a loupe diving into each.
3. A two-part film: map flight for the eras, light table for the documents.

Choice: 1. The approved script, timings and voice stay. From the style we take catalogue truth (each object
carries what its record says and the narration does not), anchor dot + leader + dark caption plate with a
signal-red edge, counters that land with the voice, lines drawn on in 0.5-0.7 s, a soft click as each label
lands, and the -14 LUFS mix target. No loupe or photograph-space depth: they cost the most and the map flight
already carries the "look closer" moves.

Also new: opening counter to 700,000, red arrows for Goguryeo's push into Manchuria and the 1592 landing at
Busan (with a wide southern view first), and an end card with title and credits.
